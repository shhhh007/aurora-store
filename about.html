The process

How AURORA works

No warehouse, no middlemen, no inflated prices. We hand-pick branded pieces from trusted suppliers, check them properly and deliver them to Germany.

The short version

You order today — in about two weeks it's at your door

We don't keep a warehouse. When you order, we buy the piece from a trusted supplier, have it quality checked, and send it straight to you. That's why prices stay fair and every item gets personal attention.

01
You pick a piece

Browse the catalog and add what you like. Every listing shows the available sizes and details of the item.

02
We confirm within the hour

You'll get a message on Telegram or WhatsApp — we confirm stock, finalize the total and send a PayPal or card payment link.

03
Quality check & dispatch

The piece gets inspected — stitching, hardware, measurements — then ships with a tracking number.

04
Delivered in ~2 weeks

Average delivery to Germany is about two weeks. We watch the tracking too, and message you the moment it lands.

What we stand for

Three promises

Checked, not promised

We don't just forward parcels. Each piece is inspected by hand before it ships — and we can send you photos first.

One honest price

Sourcing direct means no reseller chains. The price on the card is final — no hidden fees at checkout.

Always reachable

Order updates, sizing doubts, or a piece you can't find in the catalog — message us, a human replies.

Ready when you are

Pick something that glows

The catalog is open — and if you can't find your piece, just message us. We take requests.
