Our story

A brand that glows from within

AURORA started with a single hoodie sewn in a rented apartment. Today it's worn in 60+ cities — but the batches are still small.

Philosophy

We're not into clothing "for everyone". We're into clothing "for our people"

Every AURORA drop is 12 models, capped at 50 pieces each. We don't restock sold-out items and we don't run "one more batch". When it's gone, the piece has done its job.

01
Quality over quantity

320 gsm fleece, YKK zippers, double stitching. Every item is checked by hand before it ships.

02
Honest pricing

No fake "90% off" from inflated anchors. The price you see is the real price, always.

03
Community decides

Subscribers vote on the next drop in Telegram. Vote — and your piece might end up in the catalog.

Timeline

How we got here

2023

The first hoodie, at the kitchen table

Bought a roll of fleece, found a seamstress through a classified ad and sold 15 hoodies to friends. Design in Figma, packaging from the corner store.

2024

The first real drop

The "Polar Night" collection: 6 models, 300 pieces, sold out in 4 days. First reviews from other cities and a waitlist for the next drop.

2025

Order number one thousand

Our own stockroom, magnetic-box packaging with a postcard, 48-hour dispatch. Launched community voting for upcoming designs.

2026

The FW26 collection

12 pieces, the warmest lineup in brand history. You're browsing it right now — and that's the best part of the story.
