FAQ

The short version

How long does delivery take?+
Average delivery is about two weeks to Germany. Every order gets a tracking number, and you'll always know where your parcel is.
How do I pay?+
After you place an order we message you with a PayPal or card payment link. You only pay after we confirm the item is in stock.
Can I return an item?+
Yes — 14 days for a return, no questions asked, as long as it's unworn with tags attached. Message us and we'll arrange it.
