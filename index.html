FAQ

The short version

How long is shipping?+
Orders ship within 48 hours via courier or post. Most destinations take 1–7 days. Shipping is free over $70.
How do I pick a size?+
Everything is cut oversized. If you like a closer fit, size down. Message us on Telegram and we'll help with measurements for a specific model.
Can I return or exchange?+
Yes: 14 days for a return, no questions asked, as long as it's unworn with tags on. One free size exchange per order.
