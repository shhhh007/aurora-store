FAQ

Frequent questions

What payment methods do you accept?+
Pay online by card via a link from our manager, or on delivery at a pickup point. Prepayment is only needed for pre-orders.
How much is shipping?+
Courier and pickup points start at $4, post from $6. Shipping is free on orders over $70.
What about returns?+
14 days for a return if the item is unworn with tags attached. One free size exchange: we ship the new size and pick up the old one by courier.
Is this authentic?+
We are the brand: we produce small batches at our own studio. Every order ships with a branded postcard and the drop number.
When is the next drop?+
Roughly every two months. Telegram subscribers get the announcement and a closed pre-order a week before launch.
