FAQ

Frequent questions

How long does delivery take?+
Average delivery to Germany is about two weeks. Every order ships with a tracking number, and we message you when it's on the move and when it lands.
How do I pay?+
After you order, we confirm stock and send a PayPal or card payment link. You never pay before we confirm the piece is available.
What about returns?+
14 days for a return if the item is unworn with tags attached. Message us first and we'll arrange the details.
Where do the pieces come from?+
We hand-pick them from trusted suppliers and have every single item quality checked — stitching, hardware, measurements — before it ships to you.
Can you find a piece that's not in the catalog?+
Usually yes. Send us a photo or a link on Telegram/WhatsApp — we'll check availability and quote you a price with no obligations.
